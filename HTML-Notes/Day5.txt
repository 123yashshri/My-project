***what is semantic elements in HTML***
semantic elements are the html5 tags, use for special purpose. that define or justify its meaning on its own.
while <div> and <span> are non-semantic elements.

**structural semantic elements**
<header>
<nav>
<aside>
<main>
<section>
<article>
<footer>

**text/content semantic elements**
<figure>
<figcaption>
<blockquote>
<cite>
<abbr>
<address>
<time>
<mark>
<dfn>
<details> & <summary>
<data>
<meter>
<progress>

**table semantic elements**
<caption>
<thead>
<tbody>
<tr> <td> <th> <table>

**form semantic**
<label>
<legend>
<output>

**media semantics elements**
<audio>
<video>
<img>
<source>
<picture>
<iframe>
